CGP Luxury Hotel – Elegant and Modern Retreat in Hoi An

Conveniently located just 1 km from Hoi An Ancient Town and 3 km from An Bang Beach, CGP Luxury Hotel offers an elegant and modern retreat for discerning travelers. The hotel features 23 spacious, well-appointed rooms ranging in size from 35m² to 40m², each thoughtfully designed and equipped with full amenities for your comfort.

All rooms at CGP Luxury Hotel boast stunning views, either overlooking lush rice fields and distant mountains or facing the tranquil city and countryside. Each room comes with large windows and a balcony, featuring soundproofing for complete serenity during your stay. In-room amenities include air conditioning, a flat-screen TV, an electric kettle, a modern bathroom with a rain shower, complimentary toiletries, bathrobes and slippers.

The hotel offers a range of standout facilities, including an outdoor swimming pool and an on-site restaurant and café. Guests can indulge in delightful meals or enjoy a cup of coffee in a cozy atmosphere with family and friends. With its ideal location, guests can easily access popular attractions such as the Hoi An History Museum and the Hai Nam Assembly Hall, just 1.1 km away.

CGP Luxury Hotel also provides 24-hour front desk service, assisting guests with tour arrangements, ticket bookings and personal requests. Airport shuttle services (at an additional fee) and car rentals can be arranged upon request, ensuring maximum convenience for your travel plans.

At CGP Luxury Hotel, guests will not only experience luxurious accommodation but also have the opportunity to fully explore the enchanting beauty of Hoi An – a UNESCO World Heritage site renowned for its serene and poetic charm.

French-style architecture in Hoi An

The five-storey building, painted in warm sand tones with turned balustrades, arched windows and the CGP Luxury Hotel sign against the blue sky, is easy to spot. Out front, a wall of bright pink bougainvillea blooms all year – a favourite photo spot for arriving guests. At night the whole façade glows warm gold, visible from the corner of Ly Thuong Kiet street.

A bright, airy lobby

Step through the arched glass doors into a lobby of green-veined stone, mustard velvet sofas, stone-topped tables and potted plants. The cloud-marble reception desk with the CGP Group logo is where you are welcomed with a cool drink and a smile. While your room is prepared, relax with a fresh orange or watermelon juice or a hot fruit tea right in the lobby.

The pool & green courtyard

The blue-tiled pool sits in the inner courtyard, framed by a timber deck, rattan chairs and the hotel's sand-coloured façade. Ground-floor rooms open straight onto the water, and at the far end lies a leafy pergola and the garden café. Swim a few laps in the morning, dip your feet with a book in the afternoon and watch the pool glow under the lights at night – each hour has its own charm.

A caring team

Behind every fresh room is a housekeeping team that works with care: smoothing every sheet, placing each cushion just so, and restocking tea, coffee, snacks and toiletries every day. We believe true luxury lives in the small details you might not notice – but will certainly feel.

At the front desk, our team looks after you from booking to check-out: planning sightseeing, arranging transfers, recommending local places to eat or preparing a surprise for a special occasion. Just ask – we are always happy to help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the hotel located?

CGP Luxury Hotel is located at 67 Ly Thuong Kiet, Hoi An City, Quang Nam – in a quiet area close to the centre of Hoi An.

What is included in a stay package?

Every package includes accommodation and daily breakfast. Depending on the package you also get dinner, afternoon tea, airport transfers, massage, room decoration or laundry – see each package page for details.

How can I pay?

When you book on our website you pay online through the PayOne gateway by international card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB), Vietnamese domestic ATM card or QR code.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Cancellation, amendment, no-show, payment and refund conditions follow the rate plan selected at the time of booking.

Is late check-out guaranteed?

Late check-out and room upgrades are subject to room availability.

Do I need to reserve dinner, afternoon tea, massage or transfers in advance?

Yes. Advance reservation may be required for dining, afternoon tea, massage, room decoration and transfer services. Airport transfers and selected wellness services may be provided by qualified third-party providers.

Can unused package inclusions be exchanged for cash?

Package inclusions are non-exchangeable and have no cash value unless otherwise stated. Complimentary laundry and minibar follow the allowance stated in the booking confirmation.

Do you have packages for solo travellers or families?

Yes. The Solo Traveller Collection includes Solo Escape (3 nights), Solo Retreat (5 nights) and Solo Signature Stay (7 nights), and the 4-night Family Holiday is designed for the whole family.

How can I contact the hotel?

Call or WhatsApp (+84) 905 926 638, email cgpluxuryhotelhoian@gmail.com, or message us on our Facebook page. Our front desk is available 24/7.
