CGP Luxury Hotel Hoi An

Conveniently located just 1 km from Hoi An Ancient Town and 3 km from An Bang Beach, CGP Luxury Hotel offers an elegant and modern retreat for discerning travelers. About the hotel

A peaceful spot among Hoi An's rice fields

From above, CGP Luxury Hotel stands out with its sand-coloured façade, arched windows and French-style balconies, backed by green rice fields stretching to the mountains. Just 1 km from Hoi An Old Town and 3 km from An Bang Beach, it keeps the calm of the countryside while staying close to the town and the sea.

23 rooms of 35 to 40 m², many with balconies over the rice fields or doors opening straight onto the pool. Play the video to get a feel for the hotel before you book.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the hotel located?

CGP Luxury Hotel is located at 67 Ly Thuong Kiet, Hoi An City, Quang Nam – in a quiet area close to the centre of Hoi An.

What is included in a stay package?

Every package includes accommodation and daily breakfast. Depending on the package you also get dinner, afternoon tea, airport transfers, massage, room decoration or laundry – see each package page for details.

How can I pay?

When you book on our website you pay online through the PayOne gateway by international card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB), Vietnamese domestic ATM card or QR code.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Cancellation, amendment, no-show, payment and refund conditions follow the rate plan selected at the time of booking.

Is late check-out guaranteed?

Late check-out and room upgrades are subject to room availability.

Do I need to reserve dinner, afternoon tea, massage or transfers in advance?

Yes. Advance reservation may be required for dining, afternoon tea, massage, room decoration and transfer services. Airport transfers and selected wellness services may be provided by qualified third-party providers.

Can unused package inclusions be exchanged for cash?

Package inclusions are non-exchangeable and have no cash value unless otherwise stated. Complimentary laundry and minibar follow the allowance stated in the booking confirmation.

Do you have packages for solo travellers or families?

Yes. The Solo Traveller Collection includes Solo Escape (3 nights), Solo Retreat (5 nights) and Solo Signature Stay (7 nights), and the 4-night Family Holiday is designed for the whole family.

How can I contact the hotel?

Call or WhatsApp (+84) 905 926 638, email cgpluxuryhotelhoian@gmail.com, or message us on our Facebook page. Our front desk is available 24/7.

Floating breakfast – a new poolside experience at CGP

From November, guests at CGP Luxury Hotel can book a floating breakfast right in the pool. A rattan tray is prepared with freshly baked pastries, tropical fruit and a chilled watermelon juice. Just let reception know a day ahead and the next morning breakfast will be waiting for you on the blue water.

14 Nov 2026

A day cycling around Hoi An from CGP Luxury Hotel

A bicycle is the loveliest way to experience Hoi An. Starting from the bougainvillea wall in front of the hotel, you are only minutes from the Old Town, or you can turn towards the lush rice fields and Tra Que vegetable village. The CGP front desk will prepare a basket bike, a hat and a map of suggested routes for you.

Introducing our collection of 13 stay packages

CGP Luxury Hotel introduces a collection of 13 stay packages from 3 to 7 nights, grouped into Relax & Stay, Romance & Celebration, Family, Long Stay, Signature and Solo Traveller. Every package includes daily breakfast plus benefits such as dinner, afternoon tea, airport transfers or a relaxing massage.

03 Nov 2026

Three days in Hoi An: what to see and do

Stroll the lantern-lit Ancient Town at night, cycle through rice fields and Tra Que vegetable village, swim at An Bang beach and taste cao lau and mi Quang – three days are just right to feel the slow rhythm of Hoi An. Our front desk is always happy to suggest an itinerary for you.
