A honeymoon in Hoi An: unforgettable romantic moments

A honeymoon in Hoi An: unforgettable romantic moments

6 Nov 2026

Some places stay with you after just one visit, and for many couples Hoi An is one of them. Lantern light reflected on the river, yellow walls draped in bougainvillea, the click of bicycles on a village road. Everything here seems made to be the backdrop for a love story. If you are planning a honeymoon or an anniversary trip, this article suggests the most romantic moments in Hoi An and how CGP Luxury Hotel can help make your trip complete.

Why Hoi An is an ideal honeymoon destination

Unlike lively destinations, Hoi An moves at a slow pace. There are no noisy shopping malls or skyscrapers here. Instead there are small streets, rice fields and a long coastline. This is exactly what newlyweds need: time for each other, without hurry.

  • A poetic setting

    The Ancient Town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, at its most beautiful when the lanterns light up in the evening.

  • Beautiful photos everywhere

    Yellow walls, wooden doors, bougainvillea and lanterns. Hoi An is an open-air studio for wedding and honeymoon photos.

  • Town, fields and sea

    In one trip you can stroll the old town, cycle through rice fields and watch the sunrise over the sea.

  • Private and peaceful

    At CGP, only about 1 km from the old town, you are still far enough away to enjoy quiet evenings together.

8 romantic moments in Hoi An

1. Releasing lanterns on the Hoai River

When night falls, sit together in a small wooden boat drifting slowly on the Hoai River among hundreds of lanterns. Each of you releases a paper lantern and silently makes a wish. Almost every couple mentions this moment when they talk about Hoi An.

Photo: Dennis G. Jarvis / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

2. A floating breakfast on the pool

One morning, a rattan tray of pastries, fresh fruit and watermelon juice is floated right on the hotel pool. You can soak in the cool water while enjoying breakfast together. Please book with reception at least one day ahead.

3. Cycling through the rice fields at dawn

Borrow two rattan-basket bicycles from the hotel and ride out to the fields around Hoi An as the sun rises. The air is fresh, dew still clings to the rice leaves and white egrets glide overhead. A morning like this will be a lovely memory in your honeymoon album.

4. Photos by the bougainvillea wall

Right in front of the hotel is a wall of bright pink bougainvillea that blooms all year, our guests' favourite photo spot. In the old town, the yellow walls, wooden doors and lanterns are wonderful backdrops too. Shoot early in the morning while the streets are still quiet.

5. A soak in a bathtub with a view of the town

After a long day of exploring, nothing is more relaxing than soaking together in a bathtub set beside a large window, looking out at the arched door and the sunset. Bathtubs are available in the King Suite Balcony rooms.

6. Dinner by the river

Hoi An has many small riverside restaurants lit by candles and lanterns. Choose a table by the window, order a few specialities such as cao lau and chicken rice, and watch the river at night together.

Photo: Christophe95 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

7. Sunrise at An Bang beach

An Bang beach is about 3 km from the hotel. Get up early one day, cycle to the beach, sit on the sand and watch the sun rise. It is a rare moment of peace in a busy life.

8. Matching tailor-made outfits

Hoi An is famous for its tailors. You can have an ao dai, a suit or matching outfits made to wear during your trip. Tailors usually finish within 24 to 48 hours, just in time for you to take home a keepsake outfit.

A 4-day honeymoon itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrive and unwind

    Check in from 14:00; your room is decorated if you booked the honeymoon package. Swim in the afternoon, walk the old town in the evening and release lanterns.

  2. Day 2

    Floating breakfast and the old town

    Floating breakfast by the pool. In the morning, order matching outfits and visit the Japanese Covered Bridge and old houses. Soak in the tub in the afternoon. Dinner by the river in the evening.

  3. Day 3

    Countryside and sea

    Cycle through the rice fields and Tra Que vegetable village. Seafood lunch at An Bang beach. Photos by the bougainvillea wall in the afternoon.

  4. Day 4

    A slow morning

    Watch the sunrise on the beach, return for breakfast, collect your tailored clothes and prepare to check out. If you'd like to stay longer, ask reception about late check-out.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the honeymoon package?

The honeymoon package lasts 4 nights and includes accommodation, daily breakfast and couples' benefits listed on the package page.

Which rooms have a bathtub for two?

The King Suite Balcony rooms (301 and 500) have a bathtub. Room 500's bathtub is set right against a large window.

Can the room be decorated?

What is the best time for a honeymoon?

February to April brings cool weather and little rain, which is ideal. If you visit on a full-moon night of the lunar month, the old town is even more magical.
