  • Three days in Hoi An: what to see and do

    03 Nov 2026

    Stroll the lantern-lit Ancient Town at night, cycle through rice fields and Tra Que vegetable village, swim at An Bang beach and taste cao lau and mi Quang – three days are just right to feel the slow rhythm of Hoi An. Our front desk is always happy to suggest an itinerary for you.
