Introducing our collection of 13 stay packages

10 Nov 2026

CGP Luxury Hotel introduces a collection of 13 stay packages from 3 to 7 nights, grouped into Relax & Stay, Romance & Celebration, Family, Long Stay, Signature and Solo Traveller.

Every package includes daily breakfast plus benefits such as dinner, afternoon tea, airport transfers or a relaxing massage.

Thank you for choosing CGP Luxury Hotel.

Each package can be paired with a suitable room: couples may choose room 500 with its bathtub by the glass and arched windows, families room 501 with two beds and a balcony over the fields, and solo travellers will love the light-filled room 301.
