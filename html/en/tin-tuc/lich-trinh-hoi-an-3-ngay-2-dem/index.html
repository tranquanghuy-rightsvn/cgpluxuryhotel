A 3-day, 2-night Hoi An itinerary: go slow, stay well, eat well with CGP Luxury Hotel

A 3-day, 2-night Hoi An itinerary: go slow, stay well, eat well with CGP Luxury Hotel

22 Nov 2026

Three days and two nights is just enough time for Hoi An to stop being a check-in photo and become an experience with a rhythm of its own: cycling past rice fields in the morning, napping by the pool at noon, wandering the Ancient Town in the afternoon and sitting by the Hoai River at night as the lanterns light up. This is the itinerary the CGP Luxury Hotel front desk usually suggests to first-time visitors, arranged session by session so you can take it slowly without missing anything important.

Before you go

Before setting off, it helps to know a few basics so the itinerary runs smoothly. CGP Luxury Hotel is located at 67 Ly Thuong Kiet, about 1 km from the Ancient Town and about 3 km from An Bang beach. The distance is ideal: close enough to walk or cycle into town, far enough that you sleep in peace at night, away from the music and crowds of the centre.

  • Address67 Ly Thuong Kiet, Hoi An

  • Check-in / check-outFrom 14:00 / before 12:00

  • To the Ancient TownAbout 1 km, 5 minutes by bike

  • To An Bang beachAbout 3 km

  • From Da Nang airportAbout 45 minutes by car. The hotel offers airport transfers (charged); please book in advance with reception.

If your flight lands in the morning, let us know in advance so reception can store your luggage and, if a room is available, help with early check-in. While you wait, you can relax in the lobby with a cool juice or head to the garden café by the pool. Your luggage is kept safe and you can start exploring right away.

What to pack?Comfortable walking shoes, a wide-brimmed hat, sunscreen, a light jacket for the evening and a compact raincoat if you travel between September and December. Swimwear is a must, as the hotel pool is free for all guests.

Day 1: Check-in and the Ancient Town at night

  1. 14:00

    Check in and get to know the hotel

    After checking in, take a moment to open the balcony door, brew a pot of tea and let your body slow down after the journey. Rooms at CGP measure 35 to 40 m², and many have balconies overlooking Hoi An town or green fields. If you are staying in a King Suite Pool Access on the ground floor, just slide open the glass door and step onto the wooden terrace by the pool.

  2. 15:30

    A swim and a snack at the garden café

    The afternoon sun in Hoi An is often still strong, so this is the best time to head for the pool. Afterwards, stop by the café under the green pergola, order a watermelon juice or fruit tea and ask reception for a town map. They will mark the quieter streets and a few local eateries worth trying.

  3. 17:00

    Cycle into the Ancient Town at sunset

    Around five o'clock the sun softens and the yellow walls of the Ancient Town turn the colour of honey. Borrow a bicycle with a rattan basket from the hotel; about five minutes of pedalling brings you to the centre. Park near the old quarter and start walking along Tran Phu, Nguyen Thai Hoc and Bach Dang streets. The Japanese Covered Bridge, the Fujian Assembly Hall and the old houses are worth visiting if you buy an Ancient Town ticket.

  4. 19:00

    Dinner of cao lau and chicken rice

    Your first night belongs to the two dishes that made Hoi An famous. Cao lau has chewy, golden-brown noodles served with sliced char siu pork, Tra Que herbs and crispy pork crackling. Chicken rice has golden grains cooked in chicken broth, topped with shredded chicken, Vietnamese coriander and pickles. A dinner like this is filling and makes you feel you have truly arrived in Hoi An.

  5. 20:30

    Release a lantern on the Hoai River

    After dinner, head to the Hoai River. Both banks are lined with hundreds of colourful lanterns and the water shimmers with light. You can take a short boat ride and release a paper lantern with a wish. If you visit on a full-moon night of the lunar month, the town is even more special with its festive atmosphere. When you feel tired, a few minutes on the bike bring you back to the hotel and its quiet street.

Traffic in the Ancient TownMotorbikes and cars are restricted in the Ancient Town at many times of day, especially in the evening. Walking or cycling is the easiest way to get around. Keep your sightseeing ticket with you, as some sites check it at the entrance.

Day 2: Countryside, coconut forest and the sea

Day two is when you leave the Ancient Town behind to see a different Hoi An: greener, simpler and closer to the everyday life of local people.

  1. 07:00

    Floating breakfast by the pool

    If you booked the day before, a rattan tray of pastries, fruit and juice will be floated right on the pool. Many couples consider it the loveliest memory of their trip. If you prefer something more familiar, enjoy the breakfast buffet at the hotel restaurant with toast, eggs, seasonal fruit, coffee and tea.

  2. 08:00

    Cycle through Tra Que vegetable village

    From the hotel, cycle towards An Bang beach and you will pass rice fields before reaching Tra Que vegetable village. The basil, mint and Vietnamese coriander here are fertilised with seaweed from the lagoon, which gives them a distinctive fragrance. Many families invite visitors to try farming for a morning: hoeing the soil, watering with traditional twin buckets and cooking a simple meal from the herbs just picked.

  3. 10:30

    An Bang beach

    A little further on is An Bang beach, about 3 km from the hotel. The beach still feels unspoilt, with fine sand and many small beachside cafés. Rent a sun lounger under an umbrella, order a fresh coconut and watch the waves. If the weather is good, take a swim before the sun gets too high.

  4. 12:30

    Back to the hotel for a siesta

    Midday is the time to stay out of the sun. Head back to the hotel, cool off, order chicken rice or a fried-egg baguette from the CGP kitchen and take a nap. This break gives you enough energy for the afternoon and evening.

  5. 15:00

    Bay Mau coconut forest, Cam Thanh

    The Bay Mau water-coconut forest lies in Cam Thanh, about 5 to 6 km from central Hoi An. Riding a round basket boat through the coconut palms, watching the boatmen spin their baskets and listening to stories of the fishing village is great fun, especially with friends or family. Reception can call a car for you or recommend a reliable operator.

  6. 18:30

    Night market and seafood

    In the evening, try the Nguyen Hoang night market across the river, selling everything from lanterns and clothes to street snacks. If you love seafood, the restaurants on the road to the beach or around Cua Dai offer good value. Don't miss banh xeo crispy pancakes, mustard-leaf rolls and the sweet, refreshing Cam Nam corn pudding.

Day 3: A slow breakfast and gifts to take home

There is no need to rush on the last day. Check-out is before 12:00, so you still have a whole morning to enjoy.

  1. 07:30

    Coffee on the balcony

    Brew a Vietnamese drip coffee, open the balcony door and watch the early sun spread over the fields or the tiled roofs of town. Many guests say this is the moment they miss most after leaving Hoi An.

  2. 08:30

    Hoi An market and the tailors

    Hoi An market by the river is a good place to buy local specialities such as tea, coffee, candied ginger or spices. If you ordered an ao dai or a suit on the first day, now is the time for a final fitting. Hoi An tailors usually finish within 24 to 48 hours.

  3. 10:30

    One last swim and check-out

    Return to the hotel for a final swim, pack your bags and check out. If your flight is in the afternoon, reception can keep your luggage. Late check-out may be possible depending on availability, so ask a day ahead.

  4. 13:00

    To the airport

    The hotel car will take you to Da Nang airport in about 45 minutes. Book the car the day before to be sure of your timing, especially in high season.

Hoi An is at its most beautiful when you take it slowly. Don't try to see everything; save a few reasons to come back.CGP Luxury Hotel front desk

Small but important tips

  • Avoid the midday sun

    From 11:00 to 15:00 the sun is at its strongest. Rest at the hotel and go out early in the morning or late in the afternoon.

  • Watch the weather in the rainy season

    In October and November the Ancient Town may flood for a few days. Reception will keep you updated and suggest alternative plans.

  • Carry some cash

    Many small eateries and street vendors only accept cash. When booking on our website, you can pay by card or QR code.

  • Take photos politely

    Ask permission before photographing local people, especially the elderly and market vendors.

Frequently asked questions

Are 3 days and 2 nights enough to explore Hoi An?

Enough for a first visit if you focus on the Ancient Town, the countryside and the beach. If you also want to visit the Cham Islands or My Son Sanctuary, stay 3 to 4 nights.

Is CGP Luxury Hotel convenient for the Ancient Town?

Very. The hotel is about 1 km from the Ancient Town, roughly 5 minutes by bike or 15 minutes on foot.

Does the floating breakfast need to be booked?

Yes. Please let reception know at least one day in advance so the kitchen can prepare everything properly.

Can the hotel book tours for me?

Reception is open 24/7 and can help arrange tours, tickets, cars and other services that fit your plans.
