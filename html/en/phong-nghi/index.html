Rooms 101 & 102

King Suite Pool Access – Garden View

Slide the door open and step onto the pool deck

Set on the ground floor (rooms 101 and 102), this room has wide sliding glass doors opening straight onto the wooden pool deck and garden. A cloud-marble feature wall, jade stone floor and a large double bed dressed in crisp white linen with the CGP runner set the tone. The glass walk-in shower, egg-shaped toilet and round marble basin keep the bathroom modern and private.

Highlights

  • Large double bed
  • Sliding doors to the pool deck
  • Cloud-marble feature wall
  • Glass walk-in shower
  • Smart TV & vanity desk

Room 301

King Suite Balcony – City View

Windows on two sides, filled with light

Room 301 is a corner room lined with French windows overlooking Hoi An's tiled rooftops and the fields beyond. A white leather sofa at the foot of the bed, a wave-patterned blue carpet and wood panelling trimmed in gold feel elegant yet warm. The bathroom features a soaking tub and a large glass window into the bedroom – draw the curtain in the evening for a private corner to unwind.

Room 500

King Suite Balcony – City View

Classic arched windows, a balcony and a sky-view tub

In room 500, white arched windows open onto a balustraded balcony with sweeping views over Hoi An and the open sky. A cream leather headboard, fabric panels lined with gold and a generous sofa bed make it easy to relax together. Porcelain amenities, CGP-embroidered towels and premium toiletries are all prepared for you.
