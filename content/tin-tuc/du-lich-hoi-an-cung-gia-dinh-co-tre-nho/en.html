Why Hoi An suits families

  • Short distances

    The old town, countryside and beach are all close together. From CGP it is about 1 km to the old town and about 3 km to An Bang beach. No long car rides for the kids.

  • A slow pace of life

    Traffic is restricted in the old town at many times of day; the streets are small and safer for the family to walk.

  • Lots of outdoor activities

    Basket-boat rides, pottery, vegetable farming, lantern releases, swimming in the sea. All of these are very appealing to children.

  • Easy food

    Chicken rice, banh mi, mi Quang and tropical fruit are dishes children take to easily, not spicy if you ask in advance.

When to go

For families with young children, the weather is the most important factor. Hoi An has two distinct seasons: dry and rainy.

PeriodWeatherFor families
February to AprilCool, little rain, gentle sunIdeal; children can play outdoors comfortably
May to AugustHot and sunny, some days above 35°CGood for summer holidays; play early morning and late afternoon
September to DecemberRainy season; possible flooding in October and NovemberThink carefully and keep plans flexible
December to JanuaryCool, drizzlePleasant if you bring warm clothes

Summer holidaysIf you travel from June to August, plan sightseeing for early morning and late afternoon. At midday the family can rest at the hotel and swim in the pool to avoid the strong sun.

Activities children will love

Swimming at the hotel

Many children consider the CGP outdoor pool the favourite "attraction" of the whole trip. The blue-tiled pool sits in the inner courtyard, surrounded by a wooden deck and rattan loungers. The pool is free for all guests.

A basket-boat ride in the water-coconut forest

The Bay Mau water-coconut forest in Cam Thanh is a place where children laugh non-stop. The boatmen often spin their baskets, catch crabs and make toys from coconut leaves for the kids. Make sure your children wear life jackets.

Pottery at Thanh Ha pottery village

Thanh Ha pottery village has a history of hundreds of years. Here children can sit at the potter's wheel and shape a cup or a small animal from clay with their own hands. It is a meaningful souvenir they make themselves.

Farmer for a day in Tra Que

In Tra Que vegetable village, families can hoe the soil, sow seeds and water the beds with traditional twin buckets together. Then everyone cooks a simple dish from the vegetables they have just picked. City children usually love this experience.

Releasing lanterns on the Hoai River

In the evening, the family can take a boat on the Hoai River and release a paper lantern. The twinkling lanterns along both banks are sure to delight the children.

Swimming at An Bang beach

An Bang beach is about 3 km from the hotel, with fine sand and many cafés with umbrellas. Let the children swim in the sea before 10:00 or after 15:00 when the sun is gentler.

A suggested 4-day itinerary

This itinerary follows a young child's daily rhythm: outings in the morning, rest at midday and light activities in the afternoon.

  1. Day 1

    Check in and settle in

    Check in from 14:00, swim in the hotel pool, have a light dinner at the CGP restaurant and go to bed early to rest up.

  2. Day 2

    The old town and lantern release

    In the morning, visit the Japanese Covered Bridge and old houses while it is still cool. Return to the hotel at midday. In the evening, walk the old town, release lanterns and enjoy sweet soup.

  3. Day 3

    Basket boats and the beach

    In the morning, go to Bay Mau coconut forest for a basket-boat ride. Have seafood for lunch. In the afternoon, swim at An Bang beach and return to the hotel for the sunset.

  4. Day 4

    Pottery village and check-out

    In the morning, make pottery in Thanh Ha or grow vegetables in Tra Que. Back at the hotel for one last swim and check out before 12:00.

Packing list for little ones

  • Clothes: breathable cotton clothing, a wide-brimmed hat, a light jacket for the evening and a raincoat in the rainy season.
  • Swimwear: swimsuit, goggles, armbands or a life jacket for children who cannot swim.
  • Sun protection: children's sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • Basic medicine: fever reducer, probiotics, mosquito repellent and plasters.
  • Familiar snacks: a few of the biscuits and milk your child usually has, in case they are not used to new food yet.
  • Small toys: picture books and crayons to keep them busy while waiting for food or riding in the car.

Need extra help?If you need a baby cot or have special requests for your child's meals, contact reception before your trip so the hotel can check and prepare.
