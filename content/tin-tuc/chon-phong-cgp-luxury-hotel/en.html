Our rooms at a glance

CGP Luxury Hotel has 23 rooms in total, measuring 35 to 40 m². It is a modest size, small enough for every guest to be looked after carefully and quiet enough for you to truly rest. The hotel is about 1 km from Hoi An Ancient Town and about 3 km from An Bang beach, so whichever room you choose, the main sights are easy to reach.

All rooms share the same core amenities, so you never have to worry about quality when choosing a lower category. The differences lie in location, view, bed layout and a few special touches such as a bathtub or a door opening onto the pool.

  • Soft beds, white linen

    Large double or King beds with white linen, decorative pillows and CGP-embroidered towels refreshed every day.

  • Large windows, balconies

    Rooms are full of natural light, and many have balconies overlooking Hoi An town or the rice fields.

  • Fully equipped

    Air conditioning, flat-screen TV, kettle, mini fridge, bathrobes, slippers and free toiletries.

  • Good soundproofing

    Windows and balconies are soundproofed, so you sleep well even after a lively day outside.

King Suite Pool Access, Garden View

Rooms 101 and 102 are on the ground floor and are among our guests' favourites. Their highlight is a large sliding glass door that opens straight onto a wooden terrace by the pool and the greenery. In the morning, just open the door and a few steps take you into the pool for a swim before breakfast.

Inside, the headboard is clad in cloud-veined stone, the jade-green stone floor feels cool underfoot, and the large double bed is dressed in crisp white linen with a CGP-embroidered runner. The walk-in shower has a glass partition, an egg-shaped toilet and a round marble basin, clean and modern. A smart TV and dressing table make it easy to get ready before heading into town.

Who is it for?Couples who love swimming, guests who prefer not to use the lift, or anyone who wants the feeling of a small villa by the pool.

Superior Room

Room 103 is a neat, cosy room for short trips. Dark wood tones combined with a cool green stone floor create a pleasant space. Above the bed hangs an oil painting of Vietnamese rice fields, a small artistic touch full of character.

A wardrobe, a desk with a mirror, a mini fridge and a kettle are neatly arranged by the entrance. The bright bathroom has a shower, a stone basin and folded CGP towels. If you are on a business trip, need a quiet place to work in the evening or simply want a clean, pretty room to sleep in after exploring the old town, the Superior Room is a very sensible choice.

King Suite Balcony, City View

This is the room type for those who want a touch more luxury. CGP offers two styles in this category, both with a King bed, a bathtub and views over Hoi An town.

Room 301: a corner room with windows on two sides

Room 301 is a corner room with a row of French windows overlooking the tiled roofs of Hoi An and the fields beyond. A white leather sofa sits at the foot of the bed, the blue wave-patterned carpet and wood panelling trimmed in gold feel elegant yet warm. The bathroom has a bathtub and a large glass panel facing the bedroom. In the evening, simply draw the curtain for your own private corner to unwind.

Room 500: classic arches and a bathtub with a view of the sky

Room 500 has white arched doors opening onto a balustraded balcony with sweeping views of Hoi An and the blue sky. A cream leather headboard, fabric wall panels with gold trim and a sofa bed large enough for the whole family to sit and chat. The crowning touch is the bathtub set right against a large window: you can soak while gazing at the arches and the afternoon sun. Ceramic amenities, CGP-embroidered towels and premium toiletries are ready for you.

Who is it for?Honeymooners, couples celebrating an anniversary or anyone who wants to end a long day with a soak in the tub while watching the town.

Deluxe Double

The Deluxe Double category also comes in two styles, suitable for small families and couples who love bright spaces.

Room 501: two beds and a balcony over the fields

Room 501 has one double bed and one single bed with terracotta upholstered headboards. It is an excellent choice for a family of three or a group of friends. The glass door opens onto a French-style balcony with green rice fields and Hoi An rooftops in the distance. A long wardrobe, TV, tea and coffee corner and a glass-walled bathroom keep the whole family comfortable throughout the stay.

Room 502: a large glass arch, full of sunlight

Room 502 stands out with a large glass arched door opening onto the balcony, flooding the room with natural light from morning to afternoon. A double bed with a terracotta headboard, a dark wood TV wall, a tea table and a minibar with drinking water. The modern bathroom has its own window. It is ideal for couples who want a bright, airy and quiet room.

Choosing by type of trip

Still undecided? Start with one question: what is this trip for? Here are suggestions from our front desk, based on experience welcoming many guests.

  1. Honeymoon, anniversary

    Choose the King Suite Balcony

    A bathtub by the window, a private balcony and a King bed are the perfect trio for special occasions. Combine it with the Honeymoon Package or Anniversary Celebration for room decoration and a few little surprises.

  2. A relaxing break

    Choose the King Suite Pool Access

    Nothing is more relaxing than opening the door onto the pool. Swim any time of day, read on the wooden terrace and order the floating breakfast right outside your door.

  3. Families with young children

    Choose Deluxe Double room 501

    A double bed and a single bed let everyone sleep comfortably. The balcony over the fields is where little ones can watch buffaloes and egrets in the morning. See also the 4-night Family Holiday package.

  4. Business, long stays

    Choose the Superior Room

    Neat, with a desk and good value for longer trips. If you stay a week, the Work & Relax Package will help you save more.

  5. Travelling solo

    Choose Deluxe Double room 502

    A bright, quiet room with a balcony is the ideal place to read, write a journal and recharge. The Solo Escape collection offers 3, 5 and 7-night packages.

Want to be sure of the room you love?Some room types have only one or two rooms. If you want room 500 or room 101 specifically, add a note when booking or contact reception early, especially in high season and at weekends.

Shared facilities worth knowing

Whichever room you choose, you can use the outdoor pool free of charge, the garden café and the hotel restaurant. The floating breakfast must be booked at least one day ahead. Bicycles are ready for you to explore the old town or ride through Tra Que vegetable village. Reception is open 24/7 and happy to help with tours, cars and airport transfers (charged).

Frequently asked questions

Which rooms have a bathtub?

The King Suite Balcony rooms (301 and 500) have a bathtub. The other rooms have a walk-in shower.

Which room suits a family of three?

Deluxe Double room 501 has one double bed and one single bed, ideal for a family with one child.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 14:00 and check-out before 12:00. Early check-in or late check-out depends on availability.

Can I request a specific room?

Yes. Add a note when booking or call reception on (+84) 905 926 638. The hotel will do its best to meet your request.
