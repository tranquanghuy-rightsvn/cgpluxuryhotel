Hoi An's two seasons

Hoi An lies in central Vietnam and has a tropical monsoon climate with two distinct seasons. Understanding them is the first step in planning your trip.

  • Dry season: February to August

    Sunny, little rain, calm sea. February to April is cool and pleasant; May to August is hot, with some days above 35°C.

  • Rainy season: September to January

    Plenty of rain, especially in October and November when floods may occur. December and January are cool with drizzle.

Festivals and events through the year

Besides the weather, festivals are another reason to choose when to visit Hoi An. The old town is especially magical on these occasions, but also busier.

  1. Every month, 14th day of the lunar month

    Full-moon night in the old town

    On the full-moon night of each lunar month the old town has a special atmosphere: many houses dim their electric lights and light lanterns, residents set out altars in front of their doors and lanterns float on the river. It is the most beautiful time to see Hoi An at night.

  2. First lunar month

    Lunar New Year and the Lantern Festival

    At Tet, the old town is brightly decorated with flowers, couplets and lanterns. The first full moon of the year (Nguyen Tieu) brings many traditional cultural activities at the assembly halls.

  3. 15th day of the 8th lunar month

    Mid-Autumn Festival

    The old town is filled with lanterns, star lanterns and the drums of lion dances. Children especially love the atmosphere.

  4. December

    Christmas and New Year

    Many restaurants and cafés decorate for Christmas. On New Year's Eve, the old town is lively with celebrations to welcome the new year.

Book early for holidaysLunar New Year, the late-April to early-May holidays, summer and the end of the year are usually fully booked. If you travel at these times, book a few weeks ahead.

Should you visit in the rainy season?

Many people hesitate to visit Hoi An in the rainy season, but this time has its own charms. The old town is less crowded, the air is cool, room rates are friendlier and Hoi An in the rain has a quiet, poetic beauty.

What to prepare

  • A light raincoat, an umbrella and non-slip footwear.
  • A flexible plan: save rainy days for museums, old houses, cafés and cooking classes.
  • Follow the weather forecast and updates from reception on river levels.

When there is flooding

In October and November, the riverside area of the old town along the Hoai River may flood for a few days after heavy rain. CGP Luxury Hotel is on Ly Thuong Kiet street, about 1 km from the old town. In bad weather, reception will keep you updated, suggest alternative plans and help you arrange safe transport.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best month to visit Hoi An?

February to April is considered the best: cool, little rain and suitable for every activity.

When is the full-moon night in the old town?

It takes place on the 14th day of each lunar month. Check the lunar calendar before your trip to pick the right date.

Should I visit Hoi An in October or November?

You still can, but keep your plans flexible as there may be heavy rain and flooding. In return, the streets are quiet and room rates friendlier.

Is Hoi An too hot in summer?

June to August is quite hot. Sightsee early in the morning and late in the afternoon, and rest and swim in the hotel pool at midday.
